Outlook Deep Link Generator

What this tool does:
This utility converts a temporary Outlook Web URL (copied after refreshing an email) into a permanent deep link for messages stored in a shared mailbox. The generated link can be safely shared with teammates and will consistently open the exact email in Outlook Web, even across sessions or devices.
Instructions:
1. Open email in Shared Mailbox.
2. Click ... > View > Open in new window.
3. Refresh the new window:
   • Windows: Press F5
   • Mac: Press Fn + F5 ⚠️ Mac users MUST hold 'Fn' for the refresh to work!
4. Copy the URL from that window and paste it below.
